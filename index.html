Sungjae Choi

M.S. student, Electrical Engineering, KAIST

I work on 3D computer vision in the SIIT Lab, advised by Junmo Kim. My research centers on 3D Gaussian splatting and multi-view geometry foundation models: segmenting radiance fields, reconstructing from few views, and pushing feed-forward geometry transformers to long sequences without training.

I did my undergraduate degree at KAIST as well, in Electrical Engineering with a minor in Cultural Technology, and spent time at Hyundai Motor Company working on autonomous driving perception.
