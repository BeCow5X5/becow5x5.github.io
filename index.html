Biography

I am an M.S. student in the SIIT Lab at the School of Electrical Engineering, KAIST, advised by Prof. Junmo Kim.

My research centers on 3D Gaussian splatting and multi-view geometry foundation models: segmenting radiance fields, reconstructing from few views, and pushing feed-forward geometry transformers to long sequences without training. I received my B.S. in Electrical Engineering from KAIST with a minor in Cultural Technology.
